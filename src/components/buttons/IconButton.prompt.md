IconButton — a compact square button for icon-only actions in toolbars, card corners, and sheet headers.

```jsx
<IconButton aria-label="Close" onClick={close}><X /></IconButton>
<IconButton variant="outline" aria-label="Refresh"><RefreshCcw /></IconButton>
```

Variants: `ghost` (default), `outline`, `ink`, `brand`. Sizes `sm`/`md`/`lg`. Always supply `aria-label`.
