ModuleTag — a category chip in one of the six module colors; use it to label lessons, nodes, and roadmap steps by subject.

```jsx
<ModuleTag module="sql" />
<ModuleTag module="python" variant="solid">Python</ModuleTag>
<ModuleTag module="statistics">Stats</ModuleTag>
```

Modules: `business`, `excel`, `statistics`, `sql`, `python`, `powerbi`. `variant`: `soft` (dot + tint) or `solid` (full fill).
